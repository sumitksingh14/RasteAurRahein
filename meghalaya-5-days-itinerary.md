# Trip: "5 Days in Meghalaya"

## Overview
- **Dates:** Flexible (best: Oct–May; monsoon Jun–Sep gives dramatic waterfalls but heavy rain/leeches on treks)
- **Region/State:** Meghalaya, India (Shillong, Cherrapunji/Sohra, Dawki, Mawlynnong)
- **Trip Type:** Nature, Waterfalls, Living Root Bridge Trekking, Village Tourism
- **Total Budget (approx, per person, mid-range):** ₹15,000 – ₹20,000 (excl. flights to Guwahati)
- **Base Arrival/Departure:** Guwahati Airport (GAU), ~3 hrs drive to Shillong

## Day 4: Living Root Bridges & Dawki
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:30 AM | Early breakfast, depart for Tyrna village |
| 7:30 AM | Trek to Double Decker Living Root Bridge at Nongriat (~3,500 steps, 2.5–3 hrs one way) |
| 11:00 AM | Explore bridge, swim at Rainbow Falls nearby |
| 1:00 PM | Packed lunch at Nongriat |
| 2:00 PM | Trek back up to Tyrna (~2–2.5 hrs) |
| 5:00 PM | Drive to Dawki, check-in |
| 6:30 PM | Sunset at Dawki river (if time permits)|

**Location:** Nongriat Double Decker Bridge (25.2496° N, 91.7247° E); Dawki (25.1892° N, 92.0197° E)
**Alt. Option (less strenuous):** Skip the trek and instead visit Mawlynnong ("cleanest village in Asia") and its single-decker root bridge.
**Notes/Tips:** The Nongriat trek is steep with thousands of steps — wear good grip shoes, carry water and snacks. Not recommended for those with knee issues.
**Photos:** [Double Decker root bridge], [Rainbow Falls turquoise pool]
**Cost Breakdown:** Cab + guide for trek day ₹2,500 | Packed lunch ₹150 | Dawki homestay ₹1,800
