# Trip: "Char Dham Yatra Uttarakhand — 10 Days"

## Overview
- **Dates:** Yatra season typically opens late Apr/early May and closes late Oct/early Nov (dates fixed annually per Hindu calendar/Akshaya Tritiya — verify current year's opening dates before booking)
- **Region/State:** Uttarakhand, India — covers Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath
- **Trip Type:** Pilgrimage, Himalayan Road Trip, Moderate Trekking (Yamunotri & Kedarnath legs)
- **Total Budget (approx, per person, mid-range, group of 4 sharing vehicle):** ₹28,000 – ₹38,000
- **Registration:** Mandatory online Char Dham biometric registration/e-pass via the Uttarakhand Tourism portal before starting — required at checkpoints.
- **Route Sequence:** Yamunotri → Gangotri → Kedarnath → Badrinath (traditional order)

## Day 3: Yamunotri Darshan
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 5:00 AM | Depart Barkot for Janki Chatti (~40 km, 1.5 hrs) |
| 7:00 AM | Begin trek to Yamunotri temple (~6 km, 4–5 hrs; pony/palki available) |
| 11:30 AM | Darshan at Yamunotri Temple, holy dip at Surya Kund hot spring |
| 1:00 PM | Begin descent |
| 4:00 PM | Back at Janki Chatti, drive to Barkot/Uttarkashi |

**Location:** Yamunotri Temple (31.0100° N, 78.4500° E, ~3,293 m)
**Notes/Tips:** Trek is steep — pony (₹1,200–1,500) or palki (₹3,000+) available for those unable to walk. Rice cooked in the hot spring is a local ritual offering.
