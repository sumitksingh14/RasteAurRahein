# Trip: "Panch Kedar Trek — 10 Days"

## Overview
- **Dates:** Best May–Jun & Sep–Oct (post-monsoon clarity, pre-winter snow); avoid Jul–Aug monsoon
- **Region/State:** Uttarakhand, India — Garhwal Himalayas
- **Trip Type:** Multi-site Pilgrimage Trek, Moderate-to-Challenging Difficulty
- **Total Budget (approx, per person, mid-range, group of 4 sharing transport):** ₹22,000 – ₹30,000
- **The Five Kedars:** Kedarnath, Tungnath, Rudranath, Madhyamaheshwar, Kalpeshwar
- **Important Note:** This is an **ambitious, fast-paced 10-day version** of a circuit traditionally done in 12–15 days. It assumes good fitness, favorable weather, and no rest-day buffers — build in 2–3 spare days if possible, especially for the Rudranath leg (the most remote and physically demanding).

| Time | Activity |

## Day 8: Gopeshwar to Rudranath Trek (via Sagar village)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:00 AM | Drive to Sagar village (~10 km, 30 min) |
| 7:00 AM | Begin Rudranath trek (~20 km one-way through Panar Bugyal meadows, 8–10 hrs — the toughest leg) |
| 5:00 PM | Arrive Rudranath, check-in at basic guesthouse |
| 6:30 PM | Evening darshan at Rudranath Temple |

**Location:** Rudranath (30.4667° N, 79.3333° E, ~3,600 m) — **Kedar 4 of 5**
**Notes/Tips:** The longest and most remote trek of the circuit through alpine meadows and dense forest — a local guide is strongly recommended, not optional. Carry enough food/water as facilities en route are minimal.
**Photos:** [Panar Bugyal alpine meadow], [Rudranath temple carved into rock face]
**Cost Breakdown:** Mandatory local guide ₹1,500/day | Guesthouse ₹800 | Meals ₹350

## Day 10: Kalpeshwar & Return to Haridwar/Rishikesh
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 6:00 AM | Drive to Helang, then to Urgam village (~15 km) |
| 7:30 AM | Short walk (~2 km, easy) to Kalpeshwar Temple — the only Panch Kedar accessible without a major trek |
| 9:00 AM | Darshan at Kalpeshwar (cave temple) — **Kedar 5 of 5, circuit complete** |
| 10:30 AM | Begin long drive back to Haridwar/Rishikesh (~230 km, 8 hrs) |
| 7:00 PM | Arrive Haridwar/Rishikesh, departure |

**Location:** Kalpeshwar (30.5000° N, 79.4667° E) — **Kedar 5 of 5**
**Notes/Tips:** Kalpeshwar is set inside a natural cave — a serene, low-effort finish to an otherwise demanding circuit. Completing all five is considered highly auspicious.
**Photos:** [Kalpeshwar cave temple entrance], [Urgam valley terraced fields]
**Cost Breakdown:** Cab (full day) ₹2,500 pp (shared) | Meals ₹400
