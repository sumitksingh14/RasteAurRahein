# Trip: "7 Days in Kerala"

## Overview
- **Dates:** Flexible (best: Sep–Mar, cool & dry; Jun–Aug is monsoon, great for Ayurveda but wet)
- **Region/State:** Kerala, India (Kochi, Munnar, Thekkady, Alleppey, Kovalam)
- **Trip Type:** Backwaters, Hill Station, Wildlife, Beach, Culture
- **Total Budget (approx, per person, mid-range):** ₹25,000 – ₹35,000 (excl. flights)
- **Arrival:** Kochi (COK) — **Departure:** Trivandrum (TRV)

---

## Day 3: Munnar Sightseeing
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 7:00 AM | Sunrise at Top Station viewpoint |
| 9:00 AM | Breakfast, visit Eravikulam National Park (Nilgiri Tahr spotting) |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch |

| 3:30 PM | Kundala Lake — boating |

**Location:** Eravikulam National Park (10.1667° N, 77.0500° E)
**Notes/Tips:** Eravikulam park closes Feb–Mar for breeding season some years — check ahead. Book Top Station taxi permit locally if needed.

## Day 4: Munnar to Thekkady
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 8:00 AM | Breakfast, depart for Thekkady (~110 km, 3.5 hrs) |
| 12:00 PM | Check-in, lunch |
| 2:00 PM | Guided spice plantation tour (cardamom, pepper, cinnamon) |
| 4:30 PM | Periyar Lake boat safari (wildlife spotting: elephants, bison) |
| 7:00 PM | Optional Kalaripayattu martial arts show |

**Location:** Thekkady/Periyar Tiger Reserve (9.5916° N, 77.1611° E)
**Notes/Tips:** Book Periyar boat safari slots in advance, especially weekends — they sell out. Early morning slots have better wildlife sightings than afternoon.
**Photos:** [Spice plantation cardamom pods], [Periyar Lake boat with elephants on bank]
**Cost Breakdown:** Cab Munnar–Thekkady ₹3,200 | Spice tour ₹200 pp | Boat safari ₹300 pp | Kalaripayattu show ₹350 pp
