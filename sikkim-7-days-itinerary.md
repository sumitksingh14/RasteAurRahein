# Trip: "7 Days in Sikkim"

## Overview
- **Dates:** Flexible (best: Mar–Jun & Sep–Dec; avoid Jul–Aug monsoon landslides)
- **Region/State:** Sikkim, India (Gangtok, North Sikkim, West Sikkim)
- **Trip Type:** Mountain/Nature, Sightseeing, Light Adventure
- **Total Budget (approx, per person, mid-range):** ₹22,000 – ₹32,000 (excl. flights to Bagdogra)
- **Group Size Assumed:** 2–4 (shared vehicle/permit costs)
- **Permits Needed:** Inner Line Permit (ILP) for Sikkim entry; Protected Area Permit (PAP) for North Sikkim (Lachen/Lachung) and Nathula — arranged via registered travel agent, needs passport photos + ID copies.

**Notes/Tips:** Book ILP in advance through your hotel/agent; roads can be winding, carry motion-sickness tablets. Keep 4 passport photos and ID proof handy.

## Day 3: Gangtok to Lachen (North Sikkim)
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| 7:00 AM | Depart Gangtok in permit-cleared shared/private vehicle |
| 10:00 AM | Stop at Naga Waterfall & Seven Sisters Falls |
| 12:30 PM | Lunch at Chungthang (confluence town) |
| 4:00 PM | Arrive Lachen, check-in at homestay/hotel |
| 6:00 PM | Rest & acclimatize (altitude ~2,750 m) |

**Location:** Lachen (27.7167° N, 88.5500° E)
**Notes/Tips:** Network connectivity is minimal beyond Chungthang (BSNL works best). Carry warm layers — temperature drops sharply after sunset.
**Photos:** [Seven Sisters Falls], [Lachen valley]
**Cost Breakdown:** North Sikkim permit vehicle package (per person, 3D/2N shared) ₹6,500 | Lunch ₹250
