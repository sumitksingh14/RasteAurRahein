## 3. Editorial Quality & Voice Checklist (Pre-Publishing Gate)
Every newly published field note or guide must satisfy:
1. **Odometer & GPS Ground Truth:** Contains real elevation measurements, exact town-to-town distances, and verified fuel points.
2. **Snippet-Winning Answer Block:** 40–60 word direct summary immediately below the Quick Facts card answering who, what, when, where, and budget.
3. **No Commercial Fluff:** Zero sponsor talking points or vague listicle fillers. Focus on real tire pressures, water crossing timings, and local police/forest checkpoint requirements.
4. **Internal Link Mesh:** Minimum 3 reciprocal links to sister itineraries and at least 1 relevant guide hub.
5. **Downloadable Offline GPX:** Functional `.gpx` file generated and verified for offline GPS tools.
